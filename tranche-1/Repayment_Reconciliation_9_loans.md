# Repayment reconciliation: the nine "partially repaid" loans

The 30 July 2026 audit sheet listed 9 closed Polygon loans as partially repaid. It summed `LoanRepaid` event amounts, but the deployed contract emits `LoanRepaid` only when a loan closes, and that event carries only the final instalment. Earlier instalments emit no event. The sheet of 10 October 2026 reads contract storage instead, and this table checks it against the actual token transfers: every transfer into GenericFundCore (`0x4173…6756`) whose transaction carries the loan id.

Result: all nine were repaid in full, principal plus interest. No loan in the book has defaulted.
